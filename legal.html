Data sources & disclaimers

Graft GIS records are built from published public data. We report what those sources say about a parcel, cite each source, and show the date of the data we used. We do not produce modeled risk scores or predictions.

Sources and attribution

This product uses data from FEMA, NOAA and other federal, state and local agencies but is not endorsed or certified by any of them. Graft GIS is not affiliated with these agencies. Agency names are used only to identify the source of the data.

What a Graft GIS record is not

  • Not a flood zone determination. It is not a Standard Flood Hazard Determination Form, a Letter of Map Amendment, or an elevation certificate, and it cannot be used to decide whether flood insurance is required.
  • Not an insurance rating or quote. Nothing in a record is an insurance outlook or premium estimate. NOAA SLOSH surge scenarios are planning tools and are not intended for insurance use.
  • Not a prediction. Flood history describes where recorded events reached. It does not say whether or how a property will flood in the future. A property outside a recorded event boundary may still have flooded, or may flood later.
  • Not a survey, inspection or professional advice. Boundaries are mapped at the scale the source publishes them and may not match parcel or structure lines exactly. Consult a licensed surveyor, engineer, insurance agent or attorney for decisions that depend on these facts.

Accuracy and currency

Public data changes as agencies revise maps and records. Each record shows the source and data date we used. We work to keep data current but do not warrant that any record is complete, accurate or up to date. If you believe a record is wrong, email data@graftgis.com and we will review it against the source.
