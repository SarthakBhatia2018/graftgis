    For MUD districts

    Give your board the district’s flood record, parcel by parcel.

    Municipal Utility District boards answer for drainage, detention and growth — usually with consultant PDFs and county maps that don’t line up. Graft GIS puts every parcel in the district on one sourced record.

    • District-wide flood history. Every parcel inside your boundary checked against Harvey inundation and FEMA zones.
    • Board-ready exhibits. Sourced, dated reports for meetings, engineers and bond disclosures.
    • Resident questions, answered. Homeowners and agents see the same record your board does.

    For builders & new construction

    Document due diligence before the slab is poured.

    New-construction buyers ask about lots that have no sales history and no prior disclosures. Give them a documented, pre-close record of the ground itself.

    • FEMA zone and base flood elevation for the lot, from the current effective map.
    • Recorded flood history for the land before the subdivision existed.
    • Storm surge scenario from NOAA SLOSH, by hurricane category, for coastal lots.
    • Nearby environmental records from TCEQ.

    Example query
    What’s the flood history for 1234 Oak Ridge Dr, Houston 77096? I need a note for my buyer.
    graftgis.get_parcel_record{ "address": "1234 Oak Ridge Dr, Houston, TX 77096" }
    {
  "parcel_id": "071-234-000-0012",
  "fema_zone": "AE",
  "flood_history": [
    { "event": "Hurricane Harvey", "year": 2017,
      "inundated": true, "source": "HCFCD" }
  ],
  "sources": ["FEMA NFHL", "HCFCD"]
}
    This parcel sits in FEMA Zone AE and falls inside the recorded Hurricane Harvey (2017) inundation boundary published by HCFCD. Suggested note: “Public records show this property is in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area and within the area that flooded during Hurricane Harvey.”
